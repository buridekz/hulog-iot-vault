01 Project Overview & Commercial Value Proposition

Hulog is an autonomous, tamper-resistant smart parcel drop vault designed for perimeter gate grills, townhouses, and multi-tenant boarding houses. It resolves the severe "last-meter unattended delivery vulnerability" prevalent across the Philippine e-commerce market (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, J&T, SPX, Flash Express, and Lalamove).

Over 65% of Philippine e-commerce packages are now prepaid via GCash, Maya, or debit/credit cards. When recipients are absent, riders routinely leave parcels vulnerable to porch theft and torrential weather damage. Rescheduling incurs heavy return-to-sender penalties. Traditional padlocks require cumbersome physical keys, whereas static QR stickers pasted on boxes can be photographed, duplicated, or scanned days later. Hulog replaces static credentials with single-use, time-expiring (15–30 min) dynamic QR/PIN tokens. Delivery riders enter the 6-digit PIN on a 4x4 keypad with zero mobile app downloads. An onboard optical IR sensor verifies physical package placement, a door limit switch validates closure, and the token immediately self-destructs.

05 Panel Defense Inquiries & Strategic Answers

Why will couriers follow this process? Riders face zero friction: no app downloads, no accounts, and no Bluetooth pairing. Entering the 6-digit PIN takes under 3 seconds—faster than waiting at an intercom or writing a missed-delivery slip.
What if the courier enters the PIN but steals the parcel? Dual hardware validation: The optical IR beam sensor detects physical package presence inside the chamber. If the box is opened and closed empty, the system flags an anomaly. Timestamped logs provide indisputable evidence for platform claims.
How does the unit maintain security during blackouts? The 12V solenoid lock is fail-secure (stays mechanically deadlocked without electrical power). An internal rechargeable 12V Li-ion battery backup provides up to 72 hours of uninterrupted operations.

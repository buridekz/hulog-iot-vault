3. Market Sizing (TAM • SAM • SOM)

TAM: Total Addressable Market ₱38.5 Billion
Scope: Total Philippine online shopping households.
• ~11.0M active e-commerce buying households nationwide.
• Formula: $11,000,000\text{ households} \times \text{₱3,500} = \mathbf{₱38.5\text{B}}$ (~$687M USD).
SAM: Serviceable Addressable Market ₱5.25 Billion
Scope: Mega Manila & Key Metros (NCR, Cavite, Laguna, Rizal).
• Standalone gated homes, townhouses & student dorms with regular unattended delivery issues (~1.5M target dwellings).
• Formula: $1,500,000\text{ units} \times \text{₱3,500} = \mathbf{₱5.25\text{B}}$ (~$93.7M USD).
SOM: Serviceable Obtainable Market ₱35.0 Million
Scope: 3-Year Realistic Sales Goal (~0.65% share of SAM).
• 10,000 units sold via U-Belt dorms & HOA partnerships.
• Formula: $10,000\text{ units} \times \text{₱3,500} = \mathbf{₱35.0\text{M}}$ hardware.
• Plus SaaS: 1,200 dorms @ ₱199/mo = +₱2.86M/yr.

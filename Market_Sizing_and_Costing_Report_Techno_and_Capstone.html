01 Review of Chapter 3: TAM, SAM, and SOM Framework

In Technopreneurship, assessing the commercial viability of a technical innovation requires defining how large the real opportunity is and how much of it we can realistically capture. Chapter 3 outlines the three-tiered market sizing framework:

TAM • Total Addressable Market
100% Demand
The overall revenue opportunity available if our product achieved 100% market share with zero competitors and unrestricted distribution nationwide.
SAM • Serviceable Addressable Market
Target Segment
The portion of TAM that fits our specific product form factor, geographic reach, income class, and distribution channels (e.g., Mega Manila gated homes).
SOM • Serviceable Obtainable Market
Realistic Capture
The realistic market share we can capture over the first 1 to 3 years, constrained by production capacity, initial capital, and early marketing beachheads.
Engineering Rationale for Product Pricing: Market sizing cannot be separated from unit economics. Before calculating TAM/SAM/SOM value, we must determine the Target Selling Price through bottom-up expense accounting: Total Unit Cost (COGS) = Direct Materials (BOM) + Direct Labor (Assembly/QA) + Manufacturing Overhead, followed by a viable commercial gross profit markup (40%–55%).

03 Application to CAPSTONE Project: Multi-Branch Hybrid WAN Self-Healing Framework

Product Summary: An enterprise network infrastructure and telemetry software suite designed for distributed public agencies and enterprises (e.g., regional offices, BPOs). It integrates edge Prometheus probe exporters, a centralized decision controller, and automated MAPE-K closed-loop routing failover to autonomously mitigate brownouts and ISP degradation without requiring a full SD-WAN rip-and-replace.

04 Comparative Synthesis: B2C IoT vs. B2B Enterprise Infrastructure

Dimension Techno Project: HULOG (IoT Vault) Capstone Project: Self-Healing Hybrid WAN
Market Nature B2C / Consumer & Micro-Rental: High volume, mass residential e-commerce buyers & landlords. B2B / Government Enterprise: Low volume, high contract value, multi-office branch networks.
Production Cost (COGS) ₱2,000.00 (₱1,700 materials + ₱180 labor + ₱120 overhead). ₱20,000.00 per branch (₱8.5k probe + ₱5.6k engineering + ₱2.4k cloud/server + ₱3.5k field/QA).
Target Selling Price ₱3,499.00 (42.8% gross margin) + ₱199/mo SaaS. ₱35,000.00 per branch (42.9% gross margin) + ₱8.5k/yr SLA.
TAM Market Size ₱38.5 Billion (11.0M online buying households). ₱2.80 Billion (80,000 enterprise branch sites in PH).
SAM Target Segment ₱5.25 Billion (1.5M Mega Manila gated homes & dorms). ₱420.0 Million (12,000 government & BPO hybrid WAN branches).
3-Year SOM Goal ₱35.0 Million (10,000 units sold @ 0.65% SAM share). ₱10.5 Million (300 branch deployments across 3–5 agencies).
Adoption Bottleneck Gate installation physical mounting & user habit shift. Government procurement bidding cycles (RA 9184) & security approval.
Key Takeaway: Hulog relies on a high-volume, consumer distribution model where affordability (sub-₱3,500 retail) and rapid self-installation drive adoption. Conversely, our Capstone project operates on a high-value enterprise systems model where reliability, vendor-independence, and uptime preservation justify higher per-site integration pricing. Both maintain a healthy ~42% to 43% gross margin, ensuring strong long-term commercial sustainability.
